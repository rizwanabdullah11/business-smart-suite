## Module 1 — Asset Management

**Category:** Operations  
**ISO Link:** ISO 9001 / ISO 27001  
**Available In:** Growth, Enterprise

### What It Does
Track all company assets including hardware, equipment, and vehicles. Log the status, location, owner, and full maintenance history of each asset.

### Key Features
- Central asset register with custom fields
- Assign asset ownership to users
- Log location and current operational status
- Link to maintenance history and service records
- Supports ISO 27001 information asset tracking

### Who Uses It
- **System Admin** — activates and configures the module
- **Compliance Manager** — oversees asset data and assigns owners
- **Standard Employee** — logs asset status updates and maintenance events

---

## Module 2 — Audit Management

**Category:** Compliance  
**ISO Link:** All ISO Standards  
**Available In:** Starter (Basic), Growth, Enterprise

### What It Does
Schedule internal and external audits, assign auditors, record findings, raise non-conformances, and close out all actions. The backbone of every ISO audit cycle.

### Key Features
- Audit scheduler with automated reminders
- Assign lead auditor and auditees per audit
- Record audit findings and link to non-conformances
- Track open findings through to closure
- Generate audit reports automatically
- Supports internal and external audit workflows

### Who Uses It
- **Compliance Manager** — plans, schedules, and runs all audits
- **Standard Employee** — participates in audits and closes assigned actions
- **External Auditor** — reviews all completed audit records (read-only)

## Module 4 — Business Environment (Context)

### Key Features

- Built-in SWOT/PESTLE analysis framework

- Map context to relevant ISO clauses

### Who Uses It

## Module 7 — Improvement Log (CAPA)

**Category:** Quality  
**ISO Link:** ISO 9001 Clause 10  
**Available In:** Starter, Growth, Enterprise

### What It Does
Record corrective actions, preventive actions, and continual improvement ideas. Track every improvement from initial identification through to final closure.

### Key Features
- Log corrective actions (CA) and preventive actions (PA)
- Capture improvement suggestions from any employee
- Assign actions to responsible owners with deadlines
- Track status: Open → In Progress → Closed
- Full audit trail of all improvement activities
- Link CAPAs to non-conformances from Audit Management

### Who Uses It
- **Compliance Manager** — reviews, assigns, and closes all CAPAs
- **Standard Employee** — submits improvement ideas and closes assigned actions
- **External Auditor** — reviews CAPA records as evidence of continual improvement

## Module 12 — IT Risk Management

**Category:** Security  
**ISO Link:** ISO 27001  
**Available In:** Enterprise

### What It Does
Identify cybersecurity risks, manage information security controls, and maintain a risk treatment plan that satisfies ISO 27001 requirements.

### Key Features
- Information security risk register
- Risk assessment using likelihood × impact scoring
- Link risks to ISO 27001 Annex A controls
- Document risk treatment decisions and owners
- Track control implementation status
- Generate Statement of Applicability (SoA) support

### Who Uses It
- **Compliance Manager** — manages IT risk assessments and treatment plans
- **System Admin** — involved in security control configuration
- **External Auditor** — reviews IT risk register for ISO 27001 certification

## Module 14 — Document Control

**Category:** Governance  
**ISO Link:** All ISO Standards  
**Available In:** Starter, Growth, Enterprise

### What It Does
Manage all policies, procedures, and work instructions with version control, approval workflows, and access rights. The single source of truth for all controlled documents.

### Key Features
- Upload and manage all controlled documents
- Version control with full revision history
- Approval workflow (draft → review → approved → published)
- Role-based access to documents
- Document acknowledgement tracking (employees confirm they've read)
- Automatic archiving of superseded versions

### Who Uses It
- **Compliance Manager** — approves and publishes all controlled documents
- **Standard Employee** — reads and acknowledges documents relevant to their role
- **External Auditor** — reviews document register and version history

## Module 15 — Risk Register

**Category:** Risk  
**ISO Link:** ISO 9001 / ISO 14001 / ISO 45001  
**Available In:** Starter, Growth, Enterprise

### What It Does
Identify, assess, and mitigate all business risks. Full audit trail of every risk decision made throughout the year.

### Key Features
- Comprehensive business risk register
- Risk scoring: Likelihood × Impact matrix
- Assign risk owners and treatment actions
- Link risks to objectives and context issues
- Track risk treatment status
- Full audit trail of all risk decisions and reviews
- Generate risk reports for management reviews

### Who Uses It
- **Compliance Manager** — owns and maintains the full risk register
- **Standard Employee** — may contribute risk information or implement treatment actions
- **External Auditor** — reviews risk register as core certification evidence
