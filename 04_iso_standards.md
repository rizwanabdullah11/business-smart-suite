# 🏅 BSS — ISO Standards Supported

> Business Smart Suite supports **six major ISO standards** simultaneously. Each standard has dedicated modules, workflows, and evidence collection built directly into the platform — making certification achievable without external consultants or spreadsheets.

## ISO 9001:2015 — Quality Management System

### Purpose
The **world's most recognised quality standard**. Ensures consistent products and services, improves customer satisfaction, and drives continual improvement across all operations.

### Why Organisations Get Certified
- Win larger contracts that require supplier quality certification
- Demonstrate quality credibility to enterprise and government clients
- Reduce defects, complaints, and operational waste
- Build a structured framework for continual improvement

### Key BSS Modules Used

| Module | How It Supports ISO 9001 |
|--------|--------------------------|
| Document Control | Manages all quality policies, procedures, and work instructions (Clause 7.5) |
| Audit Management | Schedules and records internal quality audits (Clause 9.2) |
| Risk Register | Identifies and treats quality-related risks (Clause 6.1) |
| Improvement Log (CAPA) | Records corrective and preventive actions (Clause 10.2) |
| Objectives & Targets | Tracks quality objectives and KPIs (Clause 6.2) |
| Supplier Management | Controls outsourced processes and supplier performance (Clause 8.4) |
| Stakeholder Management | Documents interested party needs and expectations (Clause 4.2) |
| Business Environment | Records context of the organisation (Clause 4.1) |
| Training Management | Tracks staff competency and awareness (Clause 7.2) |

### Key ISO 9001 Clauses Covered in BSS

| Clause | Requirement | BSS Module |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| 4.1 | Context of the Organisation | Business Environment |
| 4.2 | Interested Parties | Stakeholder Management |
| 6.1 | Risks and Opportunities | Risk Register |
| 6.2 | Quality Objectives | Objectives & Targets |
| 7.2 | Competence | Training Management |
| 7.5 | Documented Information | Document Control |
| 8.4 | Externally Provided Processes | Supplier Management |
| 9.2 | Internal Audit | Audit Management |
| 10.2 | Nonconformity and Corrective Action | Improvement Log (CAPA) |

## ISO 14001:2015 — Environmental Management System

### Purpose
Helps organisations **reduce their environmental impact**, comply with environmental legislation, and achieve sustainability goals. Demonstrates environmental responsibility to clients, regulators, and communities.

### Why Organisations Get Certified
- Meet regulatory environmental obligations
- Reduce energy costs, emissions, and waste
- Improve reputation with environmentally-conscious clients
- Required for public sector contracts in many industries

### Key BSS Modules Used

| Module | How It Supports ISO 14001 |
|--------|---------------------------|
| Environmental Aspects | Logs environmental impacts of operations (Clause 6.1.2) |
| Legal Register | Tracks environmental laws and regulations (Clause 6.1.3) |
| Objectives & Targets | Sets and monitors sustainability targets (Clause 6.2) |
| Audit Management | Conducts environmental management system audits (Clause 9.2) |
| Document Control | Manages environmental policies and procedures (Clause 7.5) |
| Obligations Register | Tracks environmental commitments (Clause 6.1.3) |

### Key ISO 14001 Clauses Covered in BSS

| Clause | Requirement | BSS Module |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| 6.1.2 | Environmental Aspects | Environmental Aspects |
| 6.1.3 | Compliance Obligations | Legal Register / Obligations Register |
| 6.2 | Environmental Objectives | Objectives & Targets |
| 7.5 | Documented Information | Document Control |
| 9.2 | Internal Audit | Audit Management |

## ISO 45001:2018 — Occupational Health & Safety

### Purpose
**Protects workers** from work-related injuries, illnesses, and fatalities. Replaces the older OHSAS 18001 standard. Demonstrates a genuine commitment to worker health and safety.

### Why Organisations Get Certified
- Legal obligation in many industries and jurisdictions
- Reduce workplace accidents, injuries, and associated costs
- Protect employees and demonstrate duty of care
- Required for public sector and construction contracts

### Key BSS Modules Used

| Module | How It Supports ISO 45001 |
|--------|---------------------------|
| Risk Register | Identifies workplace hazards and safety risks (Clause 6.1) |
| Legal Register | Tracks health & safety legislation (Clause 6.1.3) |
| Training Management | Demonstrates worker competency and safety training (Clause 7.2) |
| Audit Management | Conducts OH&S system audits (Clause 9.2) |
| Improvement Log (CAPA) | Records incident investigations and corrective actions (Clause 10.2) |
| Document Control | Manages safety procedures and policies (Clause 7.5) |

### Key ISO 45001 Clauses Covered in BSS

| Clause | Requirement | BSS Module |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| 6.1 | Risks and Opportunities | Risk Register |
| 6.1.3 | Legal Requirements | Legal Register |
| 7.2 | Competence | Training Management |
| 7.5 | Documented Information | Document Control |
| 9.2 | Internal Audit | Audit Management |
| 10.2 | Nonconformity and Corrective Action | Improvement Log (CAPA) |

## ISO 27001:2022 — Information Security Management

### Purpose
**Protects sensitive business information**, manages cybersecurity risk, and demonstrates data security to clients and regulators. Essential for IT companies, data processors, and any business handling sensitive information.

### Why Organisations Get Certified
- Reassure clients that their data is secure
- Demonstrate GDPR and data protection compliance readiness
- Required by many enterprise and government contracts
- Reduce the risk of costly data breaches and cyber incidents

### Key BSS Modules Used

| Module | How It Supports ISO 27001 |
|--------|---------------------------|
| IT Risk Management | Manages information security risks (Clause 6.1.2) |
| Asset Management | Tracks all information assets (Annex A 5.9) |
| Training Management | Ensures staff security awareness training (Annex A 6.3) |
| Document Control | Manages information security policies (Clause 7.5) |
| Audit Management | Conducts information security audits (Clause 9.2) |
| Legal Register | Tracks data protection and privacy laws (Clause 6.1.3) |

### Key ISO 27001 Clauses Covered in BSS

| Clause | Requirement | BSS Module |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| 6.1.2 | Information Security Risk Assessment | IT Risk Management |
| 6.1.3 | Information Security Risk Treatment | IT Risk Management |
| 7.2 | Competence | Training Management |
| 7.5 | Documented Information | Document Control |
| 9.2 | Internal Audit | Audit Management |
| Annex A 5.9 | Inventory of Information Assets | Asset Management |
| Annex A 6.3 | Security Awareness Training | Training Management |

## ISO 27701:2019 — Privacy Information Management

### Purpose
An **extension of ISO 27001** specifically covering GDPR and privacy compliance for the handling of personal data. Demonstrates that privacy is built into operations, not bolted on.

### Why Organisations Get Certified
- Demonstrate GDPR compliance to regulators and customers
- Build trust with individuals whose data you process
- Complement ISO 27001 with dedicated privacy controls
- Required by some enterprise clients as a condition of contract

### Key BSS Modules Used

| Module | How It Supports ISO 27701 |
|--------|---------------------------|
| IT Risk Management | Manages privacy risks and controls |
| Legal Register | Tracks GDPR and data protection legislation |
| Obligations Register | Records privacy commitments to data subjects |
| Document Control | Manages privacy notices, policies, and procedures |

---

## ISO 22301:2019 — Business Continuity Management

### Purpose
Ensures an organisation can **continue operations during and after disruptive incidents** such as cyber attacks, natural disasters, or supply chain failures. Demonstrates resilience to clients and regulators.

### Why Organisations Get Certified
- Protect business operations from unexpected disruption
- Demonstrate resilience to enterprise and government clients
- Required in sectors like finance, healthcare, and critical infrastructure
- Reduce financial losses from unplanned downtime

### Key BSS Modules Used

| Module | How It Supports ISO 22301 |
|--------|---------------------------|
| Risk Register | Identifies business continuity risks (Clause 8.2) |
| Audit Management | Conducts BC management system audits (Clause 9.2) |
| Document Control | Manages BC plans and procedures (Clause 7.5) |
| Objectives & Targets | Sets and tracks recovery time objectives (Clause 6.2) |
| Improvement Log (CAPA) | Records lessons learned from incidents (Clause 10.1) |
