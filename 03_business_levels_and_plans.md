# 💼 BSS — Business Levels & Subscription Plans

> Business Smart Suite offers **three subscription tiers** designed to match the size and complexity of your organisation. Every plan includes core compliance modules, with higher tiers unlocking automation, advanced reporting, and dedicated support.

---

## Plan Comparison at a Glance

| Feature | 🟢 Starter | 🔵 Growth | 🟣 Enterprise |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------------|
| **Ideal For** | Small businesses, startups | Mid-sized organisations | Large / regulated businesses |
| **User Limit** | Up to 10 users | Up to 50 users | Unlimited users |
| **Billing** | Monthly / Annual | Monthly / Annual | Monthly / Annual |
| **ISO Standards** | ISO 9001 | ISO 9001, 14001, 45001 | ISO 9001, 14001, 27001, 45001 |
| **Support** | Email + Guide | Email + Phone + Onboarding | Priority Phone + Email + Dedicated Account Manager |
| **Modules** | 6 core modules | All Starter + 7 more | All Growth + 7 more |

---

## 🟢 STARTER — Best for Small Businesses

### Who It's For
Startups, micro-businesses, and sole traders taking their **first steps toward ISO certification**. Designed to be simple, affordable, and fast to set up.

### Key Details
- **Users:** Up to 10
- **Billing:** Monthly or Annual
- **ISO Standards:** ISO 9001 (Quality Management)
- **Support:** Email support + Implementation Guide

### Example Use Case
> A 10-person manufacturing firm seeking ISO 9001 certification to win larger contracts and demonstrate quality credibility to enterprise clients.

### Included Modules

| # | Module | Purpose |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1 | ✅ Document Control | Manage all policies, procedures, and work instructions |
| 2 | ✅ Audit Management (Basic) | Schedule and conduct internal audits |
| 3 | ✅ Risk Register | Identify, assess, and mitigate business risks |
| 4 | ✅ Legal Register | Track all applicable laws and regulations |
| 5 | ✅ Objectives & Targets | Set and track compliance and business goals |
| 6 | ✅ Improvement Log (CAPA) | Log and track corrective and preventive actions |

### What You Get
- Pre-built ISO 9001 templates for rapid setup
- Role-based access for Admin, Compliance Manager, and Employees
- Cloud-based access from any browser or mobile device
- Real-time task dashboard for employees
- Basic audit scheduling and reporting

### Limitations
- ISO 9001 only (no environmental or H&S standards)
- No supplier, training, or stakeholder management modules
- Basic audit workflows (no automation)
- Maximum 10 user accounts

---

## 🔵 GROWTH — Best for Mid-Sized Organisations

### Who It's For
Growing companies with **multiple departments and more complex compliance needs**. Designed to manage people, suppliers, and environmental responsibility alongside quality.

### Key Details
- **Users:** Up to 50
- **Billing:** Monthly or Annual
- **ISO Standards:** ISO 9001 + ISO 14001 (Environment) + ISO 45001 (Health & Safety)
- **Support:** Email + Phone support + Onboarding assistance

### Example Use Case
> A 50-person construction company managing multiple sites and subcontractors, needing quality, environmental, and health & safety compliance simultaneously.

### Included Modules

| # | Module | Purpose |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1–6 | ✅ All Starter Modules | Everything from the Starter plan |
| 7 | ✅ Stakeholder Management | Track customer, supplier, and regulator relationships |
| 8 | ✅ Supplier Management | Evaluate and monitor supplier performance and compliance |
| 9 | ✅ Training Management | Assign training, track completion, store certificates |
| 10 | ✅ Equipment Maintenance | Log and schedule equipment maintenance |
| 11 | ✅ Business Environment (Context) | Document SWOT/PESTLE, internal and external issues |
| 12 | ✅ Environmental Aspects | Log environmental impacts, track emissions and waste |
| 13 | ✅ Asset Management | Track all company assets, location, and maintenance history |

### What You Get
- Everything in Starter, plus:
- Multi-standard ISO management (9001 + 14001 + 45001)
- Supplier evaluation and approved supplier list
- Full training management and competency tracking
- Environmental aspect and impact register
- Equipment and asset maintenance tracking
- Phone support and onboarding assistance

### Limitations
- ISO 27001 (Information Security) not included
- No IT Risk Management or Obligations Register
- No advanced analytics or automated audit workflows
- No dedicated account manager
- Maximum 50 user accounts

---

## 🟣 ENTERPRISE — Best for Large / Established Organisations

### Who It's For
Large, regulated businesses needing **full automation and simultaneous multi-standard compliance**. Designed for complex, multi-site operations with dedicated compliance needs.

### Key Details
- **Users:** Unlimited
- **Billing:** Monthly or Annual
- **ISO Standards:** ISO 27001 + ISO 9001 + ISO 14001 + ISO 45001 simultaneously
- **Support:** Priority phone + email + dedicated account manager

### Example Use Case
> A 300-person IT firm managing GDPR compliance, ISO 27001 information security, ISO 9001 quality, and ISO 14001 environmental requirements all at once.

### Included Modules

| # | Module | Purpose |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1–13 | ✅ All Growth Modules | Everything from Starter and Growth plans |
| 14 | ✅ IT Risk Management | Cybersecurity risk register, ISO 27001 controls |
| 15 | ✅ Obligations Register | Track commitments to customers, regulators, stakeholders |
| 16 | ✅ Advanced Analytics & KPI Dashboards | Custom dashboards and compliance performance analytics |
| 17 | ✅ Automated Audit Workflows | Fully automated audit scheduling and action tracking |
| 18 | ✅ Multi-Site Management | Manage compliance across multiple locations |
| 19 | ✅ Priority Support & Dedicated Account Manager | Named account manager, priority response |
| 20 | ✅ External Auditor Access Portal | Temporary read-only access for certification auditors |

### What You Get
- Everything in Growth, plus:
- Full ISO 27001 information security management
- IT Risk Register with Annex A control mapping
- Obligations Register for contractual commitments
- Advanced custom KPI dashboards and analytics
- Automated audit workflows (no manual scheduling needed)
- Multi-site management for complex organisations
- Unlimited user accounts
- External Auditor guest portal (time-limited, read-only)
- Dedicated account manager for onboarding and ongoing support
- Priority phone and email support

## Choosing the Right Plan

### Decision Guide

```
Is your team fewer than 10 people?
  → Start with STARTER

Do you need to manage suppliers, training, or environmental compliance?
  → Move to GROWTH

Do you need ISO 27001, unlimited users, or advanced automation?
  → Go with ENTERPRISE
```

### Upgrade Path
Plans are designed to grow with your business. You can upgrade from Starter → Growth → Enterprise at any time without losing any of your existing data, configurations, or audit history.

## Billing Options

| Option | Benefit |
|--------|---------|
| **Monthly Billing** | Flexibility, no long-term commitment |
| **Annual Billing** | Typically discounted vs monthly; recommended for established compliance programmes |

---

> 📌 **Note for Developers:** The user's active plan determines which modules are enabled and visible across the platform. Implement plan-level module gating at the API level. The System Administrator can view and manage the current plan from the Billing section of the Admin Dashboard. When a user's plan is upgraded, newly unlocked modules should become immediately available without requiring re-login.
