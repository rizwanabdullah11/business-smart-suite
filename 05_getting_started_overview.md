# 🚀 BSS — Getting Started & Platform Overview

> This guide covers everything needed to understand and set up Business Smart Suite — from first login to achieving ISO certification. Use this as the primary onboarding reference for new organisations.

---

## 1. What is Business Smart Suite?

**Business Smart Suite (BSS)** is a cloud-based, integrated compliance and quality management platform designed to help organisations of every size achieve and maintain ISO certification — without the pain of spreadsheets and disconnected processes.

It centralises all compliance activities into a **single, easy-to-use system** accessible from any device, anywhere:

- 📄 Documents and version control
- 🔍 Internal and external audits
- ⚠️ Risk and legal registers
- 🎓 Training and competency records
- 🏭 Supplier and equipment management
- 📊 KPI dashboards and management reports

---

## 2. Core Mission

> *To eliminate the pain of manual compliance management by replacing spreadsheets, shared drives, and disconnected processes with smart automation, real-time dashboards, and a structured audit trail that makes certification stress-free.*

## 4. Getting Started — 5-Phase Setup Guide

The BSS onboarding process is structured into **5 clear phases**, designed to take any organisation from zero to audit-ready.

---

### ✅ Phase 1 — Sign Up & Setup

**Goal:** Get the platform registered and configured for your organisation.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Visit the platform, choose your plan (Starter / Growth / Enterprise), and register your organisation |
| 2 | Complete the onboarding wizard — enter business name, industry, and ISO targets |
| 3 | Select and activate the modules you need from the Admin Dashboard |
| 4 | Customise your brand — upload your logo and set organisational colours |

**Who does this:** System Administrator  
**Time required:** ~30 minutes

---

### ✅ Phase 2 — Build Your System

**Goal:** Populate the platform with your organisation's specific compliance data.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Use pre-built templates to generate your Quality Manual, policies, and procedures |
| 2 | Populate the Legal Register with all applicable legislation for your industry |
| 3 | Set up the Risk Register with your known business and operational risks |
| 4 | Configure the Business Environment (Context) module with internal and external issues |

**Who does this:** Compliance Manager  
**Time required:** 1–3 days (depending on organisation size)

---

### ✅ Phase 3 — Add Your Team

**Goal:** Onboard all users and configure their access properly.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Create user accounts for all employees |
| 2 | Assign roles: who is Compliance Manager, who is Standard Employee |
| 3 | Set module-level permissions per user to control what each person can access |
| 4 | Enable MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication) for Admin and Compliance Manager accounts |

**Who does this:** System Administrator  
**Time required:** 1–2 hours

---

### ✅ Phase 4 — Run Compliance

**Goal:** Activate the day-to-day compliance workflows across your organisation.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Assign training modules to employees and set completion deadlines |
| 2 | Schedule your first internal audit using the Audit Management module |
| 3 | Assign maintenance schedules for equipment and assets |
| 4 | Set Objectives & KPI targets for the year |

**Who does this:** Compliance Manager (with employee participation)  
**Time required:** Ongoing

---

### ✅ Phase 5 — Achieve Certification

**Goal:** Use the platform to demonstrate compliance and achieve ISO certification.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Conduct your internal audit and close all non-conformances found |
| 2 | Run a Management Review and document the outputs |
| 3 | Invite your external certification body auditor as a Guest user |
| 4 | Allow the auditor to navigate all evidence — everything is in one place |
| 5 | Receive your ISO certificate. Renew annually with the same workflow |

**Who does this:** Compliance Manager + System Administrator + External Auditor  
**Time required:** 1–2 days for the audit itself

## 6. Key Platform Concepts

### Audit Trail
Every action taken in BSS is **automatically logged** with a timestamp and the user who performed it. This creates an immutable audit trail that satisfies ISO requirements for documented information and traceability. No manual logging required.

### Controlled Documents
All documents in BSS go through a structured lifecycle:
```
Draft → Under Review → Approved → Published → Archived
```
Previous versions are automatically archived when a new version is published. Employees are notified of new document releases and must acknowledge they have read them.

### Non-Conformances
A non-conformance is any deviation from a requirement — whether found during an internal audit, reported by an employee, or flagged by a supplier. In BSS, non-conformances are:
1. Logged in the Audit Management or Improvement Log module
2. Assigned to a responsible owner
3. Investigated with a root cause analysis
4. Resolved with a corrective action (CAPA)
5. Closed with evidence of resolution
6. Tracked through the full audit trail

### KPI Dashboards
Key Performance Indicators are set in the Objectives & Targets module and tracked automatically. Compliance Managers can view real-time progress against all annual targets from their dashboard.

### Automated Reminders
BSS sends automated reminders for:
- Upcoming and overdue tasks
- Training modules due for completion
- Equipment and asset maintenance due dates
- Audit schedules and preparation deadlines
- Document review and reapproval dates
- Legal register updates

## 7. Security & Access

### Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
MFA is available for all user accounts and **strongly recommended** for System Administrators and Compliance Managers. Configured by the System Administrator from the Security Settings panel.

### Role-Based Access Control
Every user sees only the modules, data, and actions permitted by their role. Role permissions are set at user creation and can be adjusted at any time by the System Administrator.

### External Auditor Access
Guest accounts for external auditors are:
- Created by the System Administrator
- Read-only (cannot create, edit, or delete anything)
- Set with a fixed expiry date
- Automatically revoked when the expiry date passes
- Limited to compliance data only (cannot view billing or user details)

## 9. Annual Compliance Cycle

Once certified, BSS supports the **ongoing annual ISO compliance cycle**:

```
Jan–Mar    Set annual objectives and KPI targets
           → Objectives & Targets module

Ongoing    Manage risks, documents, training, suppliers
           → All active modules

Mid-Year   Conduct internal audit (Cycle 1)
           → Audit Management module

Ongoing    Close non-conformances, log improvements
           → Improvement Log (CAPA)

Q3-Q4      Conduct internal audit (Cycle 2)
           → Audit Management module

Q4         Run Management Review
           → Reports and Dashboards

Annual     Invite external auditor for surveillance/recertification
           → External Auditor Portal (Enterprise)
           → Renew ISO certificate
```
